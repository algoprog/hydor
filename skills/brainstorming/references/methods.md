# Idea methods

Each method with its prompts and a few ideas it gave for one running example: **"How might we bring
more regulars into the café on weekday afternoons, without extra staff?"** Use three to five methods
per session; switch when a method stops producing.

## Obvious first

Prompt: "What would anyone suggest in the first minute?"
- Afternoon discount; loyalty card; social media posts; cakes on offer; free Wi-Fi.

Write them down, then move on: they are the baseline the other methods must beat.

## Reversal

Prompt: "How could we make sure nobody comes in the afternoon?" Then flip each answer.
- Make it noisy and rushed → a quiet hour, phones on silent, soft music.
- No sockets, Wi-Fi password hard to find → sockets at every table, password on the cups.
- Hide the afternoon offer → a board on the pavement from 14:00.

## Worst idea

Prompt: "What is the worst possible idea?" Then: "What is good about it?"
- "Charge people to sit down" → a paid desk pass for remote workers: a seat, refills and Wi-Fi for a
  fixed price.
- "Lock the door at 3" → an invitation-only book club that meets at 3.

## Constraints

Prompts: "With no budget?" "If it had to work by tomorrow?" "For children?" "For people who never
drink coffee?" "If the café were ten times bigger?"
- No budget: swap shelves, a book exchange shelf.
- For children: an after-school hot-chocolate hour with homework tables.
- Non-coffee drinkers: a tea flight, a tasting board.

## SCAMPER

Apply each word to the thing that exists (here, the afternoon offer):
- **Substitute:** cake for a savoury snack; music for silence.
- **Combine:** coffee with a class (sketching, language exchange).
- **Adapt:** borrow the "happy hour" of bars.
- **Modify:** a bigger table for groups; a smaller, cheaper cup.
- **Put to another use:** the room as a meeting space for local groups.
- **Eliminate:** the queue: order at the table with a QR code.
- **Reverse or rearrange:** customers make their own drink at a self-serve tea bar.

## Analogies

Prompt: "Who else gets people to come back at a quiet time, and how?"
- Gyms: off-peak memberships → an off-peak coffee subscription.
- Libraries: quiet rules and events → a weekly author talk.
- Airlines: lounges for frequent flyers → a regulars' table with perks.

## Other eyes

Prompt: "What would this person want, or suggest?"
- A student: cheap refills and power. A retired neighbour: company and a newspaper. A competitor:
  "I'd worry if they started delivering to offices."

## Random input

Take a random word (open a book, pick a noun) and force a link. "Lighthouse" → a lamp in the window
that turns on when the afternoon offer starts; a "safe harbour" quiet zone.

## Combinations (morphological box)

| Who | What | When | How it is offered |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| students | refills | 14:00–17:00 | a pass |
| remote workers | a meeting table | Wednesdays | a booking |
| parents | a class | after school | a membership |

Pick one cell from each column: "remote workers + a meeting table + Wednesdays + a booking".

## Crazy eights

For a person or a group: fold a sheet into eight boxes, one sketch or idea per box, a minute each,
no stopping. For you: take the strongest idea so far and write eight quick variations of it (cheaper,
bigger, for another group, at another time, online, as a subscription, as an event, as a product).

## Narrowing: a scoring table

| Idea | Impact (1–5) | Effort (1–5, 5 = easy) | Speed to try (1–5) | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Desk pass for remote workers | 4 | 3 | 4 | 11 |
| Wednesday study table | 3 | 5 | 5 | 13 |
| Self-serve tea bar | 3 | 2 | 2 | 7 |

Scores sort; they do not decide. Read the top of the list again and ask whether it feels right.

## Running a session for a group (45 minutes)

1. The question and limits on the wall (3 min).
2. Alone, silently, ideas on notes (7 min): it stops the loudest voice setting the direction.
3. Share and cluster (10 min).
4. Two methods as a group, for example reversal and analogies (15 min).
5. Dot vote, three dots each (5 min).
6. For the top three: owner, smallest test, date (5 min).
