# Source notes

One line per source while you work; they become the citations at the end.

```markdown
- [<title>](<address>), <publisher or author>, <date>. Says: <the claim, with the number or quote>.
  Kind: primary | secondary | opinion. Notes: <how it was measured, who paid, how it differs>.
```

## Quick checks of a source

- **Who:** an author or organisation you can name; their stake in the answer.
- **When:** the date of the page and of the data in it.
- **Where from:** does it cite where its numbers come from? Follow the trail to the first source when
  a number matters.
- **Agreement:** does an independent source say the same? If not, why might they differ?
- **Fit:** does it answer this question, for this place, time and definition?

## In the answer

- Key claims carry a citation right where they are made.
- Disputed points quote the exact words.
- A short list of sources at the end, in the order they were cited.
