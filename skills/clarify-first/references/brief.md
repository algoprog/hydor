# The brief

Short and plain. Fill in what applies; leave out empty headings.

```markdown
# Brief: <what it is, in a few words>

**Goal.** What it should achieve, and why now. One or two sentences.

**For.** Who will use, read, attend or live with it, and what they need from it.

**In scope.** What will be made or done.
**Out of scope.** What will not, so nobody expects it.

**Limits.** Budget, dates, deadlines, size or length, tools or materials to use or avoid, rules.

**Approach.** The option chosen, in two or three sentences, and why.

**Deliverables.** Exactly what they will get: files, formats, a plan, a list, a booked thing.

**Done when.** How everyone will know it is finished and right.

**My calls.** Decisions I made myself, each with a word on why. Change any of them.
- …

**Open.** Questions still waiting, and what happens if they stay open.
```

Then ask: "Shall I go ahead with this, or change something first?"
