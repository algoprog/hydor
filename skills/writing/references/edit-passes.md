# Edit passes

Each pass looks for one kind of problem. Doing them separately catches far more than one read
"fixing everything".

## 1. Purpose

- Reread the brief. Would this reader, after reading, know, decide or do what they should?
- Cut sections, paragraphs and asides that do not serve that, even good ones.
- Is anything the reader needs missing?

## 2. Structure

- Could someone read only the first paragraph, or only the headings, and get the point?
- Is each section in the order the reader needs it? Are there jumps the reader cannot follow?
- Headings say what the section contains ("Costs rise in March"), not just its topic ("Costs").

## 3. Paragraphs and sentences

- One idea per paragraph, stated early. Each paragraph does a job (a fact, a cause, an option, a
  consequence, an ask): cut those that only repeat the conclusion, praise the idea or announce what
  comes next.
- Sentences over about 25 words: split them, or make sure they read easily.
- Cut empty openers ("It is important to note that"), stacked hedges ("might possibly"), and repeats.
- Vary the length: a short sentence after long ones lands.

## 4. Words

- Plain words: "use" for "utilise", "about" for "with regard to".
- One term for one thing throughout; define it once if the reader may not know it.
- Verbs over nouns made from verbs: "decide" rather than "make a decision".
- Say who does what: "the on-duty agent takes the next request" tells the reader more than
  "streamlined intake unlocks efficiency".

## 5. Facts

- Every name, number, date, quote and link checked against its source.
- Numbers add up; units and currencies are stated; dates have years where it matters.
- A number keeps its base and period ("18 of 60 requests, over four weeks"); three customers asking
  never becomes "customers overwhelmingly want".
- Claims that need a source have one.

## 6. Polish

- Spelling, grammar, punctuation; consistent capitals, lists and number formats.
- Formatting fits the medium: headings and lists for documents, short paragraphs for email.
- Length fits what was asked.
