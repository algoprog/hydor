# Widths of the common templates

Points are TeX points (72.27 to the inch). Measure your own template when it is not here
(`\showthe\columnwidth`, `\showthe\textwidth`): templates change between years.

| Template | One column | The page | Its font |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ACL, EMNLP, NAACL (acl.sty) | 219.1 pt, 3.03 in | 455.2 pt, 6.30 in | Times |
| ACM sigconf (SIGIR, KDD, CIKM, WSDM, TheWebConf) | 241.1 pt, 3.33 in | 506.3 pt, 7.00 in | Linux Libertine |
| IEEE conference (IEEEtran) | 252.0 pt, 3.49 in | 516.0 pt, 7.14 in | Times |
| ICML | 234.8 pt, 3.25 in | 487.8 pt, 6.75 in | Times |
| NeurIPS, ICLR (one column) | 397.5 pt, 5.50 in | 397.5 pt, 5.50 in | Times |
| Springer LNCS (one column) | 347.7 pt, 4.81 in | 347.7 pt, 4.81 in | Computer Modern |
| Elsevier (elsarticle, one column) | 360.0 pt, 4.98 in | 360.0 pt, 4.98 in | Computer Modern |
| A thesis on A4, 3 cm margins | about 426 pt, 5.9 in | the same | the thesis's |
