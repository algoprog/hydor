# Meeting recordings

Turns a recording of a meeting, interview or call into notes: a summary, the decisions, action items
with owners and dates, open questions and a draft follow-up email, each point with the time in the
recording where it was said, so anyone can check it. It writes down only what was said: proposals
stay apart from decisions, an owner or a date only when someone agreed to it, private remarks stay
out, and nothing is sent to anyone without asking you first.

It builds on the Speech to text plugin (which builds on Audio and video): both are added with it, and
the recording is transcribed on this computer.
