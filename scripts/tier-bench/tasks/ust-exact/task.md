src/linkload.mjs estimates, for each link of our mesh network, how likely it is to be part of the spanning tree our routing protocol uses. The protocol picks a random spanning tree with probability proportional to the product of its links' qualities. The estimate comes from sampling 20,000 trees, so it is slow and only good to about two decimal places, and the new dashboard needs every link's probability to within 1e-8, for networks of up to 400 nodes and 4,000 links, in under two seconds. Please replace the estimate with an exact computation. Keep `linkProbabilities(nodes, links)` and what it returns, as the comment at the top of src/linkload.mjs describes. Plain Node.js 22, no packages.
