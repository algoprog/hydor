I have been thinking about the kiln page. When someone puts their name down I would also like to know how many shelves they want, from one to four. They have to tell me, and nobody should be able to ask for more than four, because that is about all I could give one person in a firing.

# What the maker says, if asked

- **The new question:** how many shelves they would like, a whole number from 1 to 4, needed.
- **Anything else:** nothing else changes. The page should say that they can ask for up to four shelves.
- **Anything else not covered here:** the maker says: you decide.
