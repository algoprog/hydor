# What the maker says, if asked

- **Name of the thing:** Hatch End Kiln Share.
- **What do you want to know about the people who put their name down?** Their name, and what they would want to fire. I must have their name. What they would fire is nice to know, but they can leave it out.
- **Other pages:** none, one page is enough.
- **Who needs to log in:** only me. Nobody else should be able to make an account.
- **Colour:** terracotta, an orange-brown, roughly #b45309.
- **Prices and dates:** a shelf is £18. Twelve shelves a firing. A firing about every second Thursday. Nothing else is decided yet.
