# What the maker says, if asked

- **Name of the thing:** Kovač Translations.
- **What do you call the two lists?** Clients and Jobs.
- **Clients:** name (needed); country (optional, just the name of the country); notes (optional, can be long).
- **Jobs:** which client (needed, one of my clients); what the job is called (needed); words (needed, a whole number); fee in euros (needed); the day it is due (needed); the day I delivered it (empty until I have); the file the client sent (optional, any kind of file).
- **Invoiced this month:** the fees of jobs whose delivery day is in this calendar month.
- **Open jobs:** jobs with no delivery day yet.
- **Average length:** the average number of words per job, over all my jobs.
- **Who sees what:** each person signed in sees only their own clients and jobs, never anybody else's. Nobody who is not signed in sees anything.
- **Anything else not covered here:** the maker says: you decide.
