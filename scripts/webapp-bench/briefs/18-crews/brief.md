I run Kestrel Landscapes, a gardening firm in Perth with about six crews who each look after their own area, and at the moment everything is in one shared notebook, so crews read each other's customers. I want a tool where each crew sees only its own: its customers, its jobs and its job notes. Anyone can start a crew by making an account and creating one, and then they are its lead.

A lead can invite people into the crew by sending them a link, and chooses whether they come in as a lead or as an ordinary member, and a lead can take people out again. Ordinary members can add and change customers, jobs and notes but cannot invite or remove anyone. Some of our people work for two crews in a week, so someone has to be able to belong to both and see both crews' things. When a person is taken out of a crew they must lose access to it at once, not at the end of the day.
