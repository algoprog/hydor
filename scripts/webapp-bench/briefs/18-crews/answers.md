# What the maker says, if asked

- **Name of the thing:** Kestrel Crews.
- **What do you call the three lists?** Customers, Jobs and Notes.
- **Customers:** name (needed), address (optional, can be long).
- **Jobs:** title (needed), the day it is for (needed), done (yes or no, starts as no).
- **Notes:** a title (needed) and the note (optional, can be long).
- **A crew:** has a name (needed). Whoever creates it is its lead. Every customer, job and note belongs to one crew, which is the crew it is added to.
- **Roles:** lead or member. A lead invites, takes people out, and can do everything a member can. A member adds and changes customers, jobs and notes of the crew and nothing else.
- **Invitations:** the lead gives the person's email and the role; the app makes a link; the person uses the link while signed in (they make an account first if they have none) and is in the crew with that role. A link can be used once.
- **Two crews:** someone can be in as many crews as they are invited to. They see the rows of every crew they are in, and may add to any of them. They cannot add to a crew they are not in.
- **Taking someone out:** takes effect on their next request.
- **Who sees what:** nobody sees a crew's rows unless they are in the crew, and a visitor sees nothing.
- **Anything else not covered here:** the maker says: you decide.
