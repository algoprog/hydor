# What the maker says, if asked

- **Name of the thing:** Totnes Seed Calendar.
- **What I put in:** the date of the last frost (a real date, like 2027-04-23, needed), and the crops (at least one) from these five: Tomatoes, Runner beans, Carrots, Lettuce, Leeks.
- **The crops, in weeks from the last frost:**
  - Tomatoes: sow indoors 8 weeks before; plant out 1 week after.
  - Runner beans: sow outside 2 weeks after.
  - Carrots: sow outside 3 weeks before; sow outside again 3 weeks after.
  - Lettuce: sow outside 4 weeks before, and again every 3 weeks, four sowings in all.
  - Leeks: sow indoors 10 weeks before; plant out 4 weeks after.
- **Saturdays:** every job is on a Saturday. A date that is not a Saturday is moved to the Saturday before it. A date that is a Saturday stays.
- **What I get back:** one line for each job: the crop, what to do ("sow indoors", "plant out", "sow outside", "sow outside again") and the date written like 2027-04-17, in date order; two jobs on the same day, the crop's name in alphabetical order.
- **If I put in something that makes no sense** (a date that does not exist, no crops, a crop that is not one of the five): it should say so in a sentence and give no list.
- **Who needs to log in:** nobody. Nothing is kept.
- **Colour:** leafy green, no particular one.
- **Anything else not covered here:** the maker says: you decide.
