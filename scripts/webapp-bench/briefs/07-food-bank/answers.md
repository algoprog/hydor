# What the maker says, if asked

- **Name of the thing:** Eastgate Food Bank.
- **What do you call the two lists?** Stock (the things in the store) and Deliveries.
- **Stock, one by one:** what it is (needed), what sort of thing it is (needed): tinned, fresh, dry goods, or baby & household; how many we have (needed, a whole number); best-before date (optional); a photo (optional, pictures only); notes (optional, can be long).
- **Deliveries, one by one:** which stock item came (needed, pick it from the stock list); who it came from (needed, a name); the day it arrived (needed); kilos (needed, a whole number of kilos); whether it has been put away (yes or no, it starts as no).
- **Running low means:** fewer than 10 of something left.
- **"This month" means:** the calendar month, by the day a delivery arrived.
- **Who sees what:** everyone who is signed in sees and changes all of it. Nobody who is not signed in sees anything.
- **Anything else not covered here:** the maker says: you decide.
