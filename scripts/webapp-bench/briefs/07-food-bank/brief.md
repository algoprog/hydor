I coordinate the Eastgate food bank. Right now our stock is on a whiteboard and our deliveries are in a notebook, and I am the only person who can read my own writing. I would like something all the volunteers can log into, on a phone if possible, so we can keep track of what is in the store and what has come in.

For each thing in the store I want to write down what it is, what sort of thing it is, how many we have, the best-before date if it has one, a photo if someone takes one so people can recognise it, and any notes. Then for each delivery I want to record what came, who it came from, the day it arrived, how many kilos it weighed, and whether it has been put away yet. When I open it I want to see straight away how many things are running low, how many kilos we have taken in this month, and how many deliveries have still not been put away. Everyone who logs in should see the same things, of course, it is one team.
