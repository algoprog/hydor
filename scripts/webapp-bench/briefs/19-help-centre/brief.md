I run a small boiler servicing firm in Harrogate and customers keep ringing me with the same questions, so I want a help centre on our website. I will write about fifty short articles myself, each in one of five groups (Heating, Hot water, Payments, Appointments and Safety), and anyone should be able to read them and search them. I want to save an article as a draft and publish it later, and a draft must never show to the public: not in a search, not on a group's page, not at its own address, nowhere.

Each article should have its own address made from its title, in lower case with the words joined by hyphens, and the address must not change if I fix the title afterwards, because people bookmark them. There should be a search box: people type some words and see the articles that have all those words in the title or the text. The ones with all the words in the title come first, then the others, each lot in alphabetical order by title. Each group has its own page listing its articles in alphabetical order. Only I log in. It is called Fenwick Help.
