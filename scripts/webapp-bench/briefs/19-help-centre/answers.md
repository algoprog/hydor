# What the maker says, if asked

- **Name of the thing:** Fenwick Help.
- **An article:** title (needed), text (needed, can be long), group (needed): Heating, Hot water, Payments, Appointments or Safety; status (draft or published, it starts as a draft).
- **The address:** the title in lower case, with every run of anything that is not a letter or a digit turned into one hyphen and no hyphen at the ends ("How we check the flue" is "how-we-check-the-flue"). It is made when the article is made and never changes after, whatever happens to the title.
- **Reading:** published articles are public; each shows its title and its text at its address. A draft's address shows nothing (it is not found).
- **Search:** words are matched whole, in any capitals; an article is found when every word of the query is in its title or its text; the title group first, then the rest; each group in alphabetical order by title (ignoring capitals). Drafts are never found. A search with no match says so and lists nothing.
- **A group's page:** lists the published articles of the group in alphabetical order by title, and nothing else.
- **Who may write:** only me. Nobody else makes an account. A visitor can read and search and nothing else.
- **Anything else not covered here:** the maker says: you decide.
