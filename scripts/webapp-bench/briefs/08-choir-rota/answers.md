# What the maker says, if asked

- **Name of the thing:** Ashby Village Choir.
- **What do you call the three lists?** Singers, Rehearsals, and Jobs (the rota).
- **Singers:** name (needed); voice (needed): soprano, alto, tenor, bass, or not sure yet; phone number (optional); the day they joined (optional); notes (optional, can be long).
- **Rehearsals:** the date (needed); where (needed, just the name of the place); how many people came (a whole number, optional because we only know it afterwards); who led it (optional, one of the singers); notes (optional, can be long).
- **Jobs:** which rehearsal (needed); which singer (needed); what job (needed): unlock the hall, make the tea, bring cake, or put out chairs; done (yes or no, starts as no).
- **The two numbers on the front page:** jobs that are not done yet, and the average number of people who came to a rehearsal.
- **Who sees what:** everyone signed in sees and changes everything. Nobody else sees anything.
- **Anything else not covered here:** the maker says: you decide.
