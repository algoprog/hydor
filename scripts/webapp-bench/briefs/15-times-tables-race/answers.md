# What the maker says, if asked

- **Name of the thing:** Times Tables Race.
- **The ten questions, always in this order:** 6 × 7, 8 × 9, 7 × 7, 12 × 4, 9 × 6, 11 × 8, 3 × 12, 5 × 9, 7 × 8, 12 × 12.
- **What a child gives:** their name (needed, up to 20 letters, capitals and spaces at the ends do not matter), their ten answers (a whole number each, or nothing for one they did not answer), and how many seconds they took (a number, 0 or more).
- **Points:** one for each right answer. Getting all ten right is worth 3 more. Finishing in under 90 seconds with at least 8 right is worth 2 more. (The bonuses add up: all ten right in under 90 seconds is 15.)
- **What they get back:** how many they got right and their points.
- **The score board:** the five best players, best first. Each name is there once, with that child's best play: more points, or if the points are the same, fewer seconds. "amy" and "Amy " are the same child. Two children with the same points: the one who took fewer seconds is higher; if that is level too, the one who played first. It shows the name, the points and the seconds. It must still be there tomorrow, so it is kept.
- **If a child gives something that makes no sense** (no name, not ten answers, a word instead of an answer, minus seconds): it should say so in a sentence and not count it.
- **Who needs to log in:** nobody.
- **Colour:** bright and a bit playful, no particular one.
- **Anything else not covered here:** the maker says: you decide.
