I am the chair of Quayside Rowing Club in Bristol, about sixty members, and everything runs on a spreadsheet and a WhatsApp group that nobody can follow. I would like a website where people join the club by making an account, put in their own details (their name, a phone number, and who to ring in an emergency), and pay their subscription, which is £15 a month, through a card payment service. Each member should see only their own details.

Two helpers need a bit more. Dee, our treasurer, should see everybody's details and who has paid this month, but not change anything. Kofi, our coach, should be able to post notices about outings and training times. Only members who have paid this month should be able to read the notices, though Dee, Kofi and I can always read them. I will make Dee and Kofi helpers myself, nobody else should be able to. There should be a public page saying what the club is, that we row on Bristol harbour from Hotwells, what the fee is, and that new members are welcome.
