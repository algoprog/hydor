# What the maker says, if asked

- **Name of the thing:** Quayside Rowing Club.
- **Who may make an account:** anyone. I, the chair, will be the first to make one, and I have all the power.
- **A member's details:** full name (needed), phone number (optional), who to ring in an emergency (needed, a name and a number written as text). Everybody keeps their own. Nobody else sees them, apart from Dee and me.
- **The two helpers:** the treasurer and the coach. I give a person the role of treasurer or coach; nobody else can give roles. The treasurer sees everybody's details and, next to each member, whether they have paid this month, and cannot change or remove anything. The coach posts notices and sees only their own details like any member.
- **Notices:** a title (needed) and a message (needed, can be long). Only the coach and I post them; an ordinary member and the treasurer cannot. They are read by the coach, the treasurer, me, and any member who has paid this month.
- **"Paid this month":** the calendar month. The card payment service tells the app "this person has paid for this month"; having paid for last month does not count. The service must not be believed unless it proves who it is (a shared secret).
- **The public page:** it says what the club is, that we row on Bristol harbour from Hotwells, £15 a month, and that new members are welcome.
- **Anything else not covered here:** the maker says: you decide.
