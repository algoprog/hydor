# What the maker says, if asked

- **Name of the thing:** Ash Physiotherapy.
- **Appointments:** 30 minutes. The slots of a day are every half hour from the start of that weekday's hours, the last one ending at the end of the hours (Monday 09:00 to 12:00 gives 09:00, 09:30, 10:00, 10:30, 11:00 and 11:30).
- **Hours:** for each weekday I give the day, a start and an end, written like 09:00 (needed, all three). A weekday I give no hours for has no slots.
- **Days off:** I give a day (needed). A day off has no free slots and cannot be booked, and the weekday's hours stay as they are for other days.
- **A booking:** the day (written like 2031-07-14), the time (written like 09:30, the start of a free slot), the visitor's name (needed) and email (needed, it must be an email address). A time that is not the start of a free slot (09:15, or after the hours, or on a day with no hours or a day off) is refused with a sentence.
- **Once only:** a slot that has been booked is not offered again and cannot be booked again, however many people ask at once.
- **Cancelling:** only I can; the slot is free again.
- **Time zone:** the UK, Europe/London. Times in the page are written in UK time in summer and in winter.
- **Who sees what:** a visitor sees the free slots and nothing of other people's bookings; only I see the bookings. Nobody else makes an account.
- **Anything else not covered here:** the maker says: you decide.
