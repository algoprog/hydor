I bake sourdough to order from my kitchen in Frome, and every time I scale a batch up or down I do the sums on the back of an envelope. I have ruined two batches that way. I want a page where I put in how many loaves I am making and how much each one should weigh, and it tells me how much flour, water, salt and starter to weigh out.

I work the baker's way, everything as a percentage of the flour: usually 72 percent water, 2 percent salt and 20 percent starter, but sometimes I want to change those. My starter is half flour and half water, so it counts towards the flour and the water in the loaf, which is the bit I always get wrong. Nobody else will use it, so no logins. I open it on my phone in the kitchen with floury fingers, so it should be easy to read. It is called Dough Sums.
