# What the maker says, if asked

- **Name of the thing:** Dough Sums.
- **What I put in:** the number of loaves (a whole number, 1 to 60, needed); the weight of one loaf in grams (a whole number, 200 to 1500, needed); the water in percent, the salt in percent and the starter in percent (each optional; if I leave one out it is 72, 2 and 20). Water may be 50 to 100, salt 0 to 5, starter 0 to 50, and any of them may have a decimal point (2.5 percent salt).
- **What I get back:** the flour, the water, the salt and the starter, each in grams, and the total dough in grams (the loaves times the weight of one).
- **The sums:** the total dough is all of it: the flour, the water and the salt, with the starter's flour and water already inside the flour and the water. The percentages are of all the flour, the starter's flour included. So the flour is the total dough divided by (1 plus the water plus the salt, as fractions), the starter is its percent of that flour, and what I weigh out as "flour" and "water" is what is left after the starter's half and half has been taken off each.
- **Rounding:** flour, water and starter to the nearest gram; salt to the nearest tenth of a gram; the total dough as it is.
- **If I put in something that makes no sense** (no loaves, a loaf of half a gram, 150 percent water, words instead of numbers): it should say so in a sentence and give no answer.
- **Who needs to log in:** nobody, it is only for me.
- **Colour:** no idea, something warm and floury.
- **Anything else not covered here:** the maker says: you decide.
